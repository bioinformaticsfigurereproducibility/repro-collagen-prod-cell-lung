# Reproducing Figure 5: Identify Collagen-Producing Cells within Normal & Fibrotic Lungs

## Source Publication

**Citation:**
Tsukui, T., Sun, K. H., Wetter, J. B., Wilson-Kanamori, J. R., Hazelwood, L. A., Henderson, N. C., Adams, T. S., Schupp, J. C., Poli, S. D., Rosas, I. O., Kaminski, N., Matthay, M. A., Wolters, P. J., & Sheppard, D. (2020). "Collagen-producing lung cell atlas identifies multiple subsets with distinct localization and relevance to fibrosis." *Nature communications*, 11(1), 1920. doi: [10.1038/s41467-020-15647-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-15647-5). PMID:32317643, PMCID:[PMC7174390](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7174390/)

**Abstract Summary:**
The study performed scRNA-sequencing to identify collagen-producing cells within normal and fibrotic (Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis & Scleroderma) lungs in humans and mouse. The study characterized the expression of Cthrc1 (collagen triple helix repeat containing 1), which was seen in fibrotic lungs and was found to produce high levels of collagen in mouse lungs. In humans, there was similar heterogeneity and CTHRC1-expressing fibroblasts present.
